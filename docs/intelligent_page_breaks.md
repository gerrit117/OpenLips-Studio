# Intelligent page breaks

TXT import keeps the source's notes, lyrics, page flags and numeric page
switches unchanged. **Intelligent page breaks**, available in the top
toolbar and Tools menu, is a separate explicit operation. The entire change
is one undo/redo step. Reapplying the same operation is deterministic.

The planner evaluates the full passage with dynamic programming rather
than cutting when a counter reaches a limit. Candidate boundaries are at
complete word groups, including continuation notes. Overlapping groups
cannot be separated into different pages. Pauses, sentence punctuation
and existing page boundaries lower a candidate's cost. Cuts immediately
after common English/German articles, conjunctions or prepositions are
discouraged, not forbidden. These are lightweight text hints, not full
linguistic understanding or an AI-generated transcription.

Page cost considers character count, note count, note span and the shortest
note's share of that span as a visibility proxy. Targets derive from the
[original-song corpus](lyric_page_corpus.md): 128 parsed distinct pairs,
11,397 normal pages and 52,900 notes, deduplicated from 211 physical pairs.
Median normal page: 16 characters, 5 notes, 1.447 seconds of note span;
95th percentiles: 30 characters, 8 notes, 2.492 seconds. The shortest-note
share's 10th percentile is 0.0673. A 3-second span target rounds out the
readability envelope; it is not a hard format limit.

Targets and scoring weights have different confidence levels:

- Measured counts/distributions: high within the supported corpus, with
  the reconstruction exceptions documented in the linked report.
- Practical scoring weights and linguistic rules: medium/experimental.
  They are manually chosen, not learned from the original charts.
- Actual native pixel widths and agreement with an original chart author's
  intent: unverified until controlled game playback and human review.

The algorithm may add or remove imported boundaries. For this explicit
reflow, interior numeric switch times are regenerated by the existing
writer; undo restores the old timings and boundaries. It preserves note
IDs, starts, lengths, pitches, text and word-end flags. It does not stretch
notes, merge pitch changes, move lyrics or change media offsets.

An unusually large indivisible word/melisma stays intact and is counted
in the result as an oversized word group. Pauses may be inferred from the
chart even without audio; incorrect note timings therefore affect the
result. There is no assumption that UltraStar BPM implies reliable meter
or downbeat positions. No beat-grid or acoustic phrase detection is
claimed. No model download, GPU, network request or new dependency is used.

Candidate pages are bounded to 64 word groups for predictable computation;
an indivisible group can contain more notes than the soft note target.
The earlier adjustable **Optimize lyric pages** tool remains available for
users who specifically want limits while retaining all existing boundaries.

Synthetic tests cover source-import preservation, pause/punctuation
preference, word/melisma integrity, overlaps, oversized groups, repeatability,
unchanged musical values, generated IXB output and toolbar undo/redo.
